About the firm

Two partners, one standard of work

Elite Finance Solutions was founded in Cairo in 2022 by two professionals who had spent more than two decades each in Big Four practice and in senior in-house finance leadership across the Middle East, Europe and Africa.

Why the firm exists

We kept meeting the same situation from both sides of the table. As buyers of professional services inside multinational groups, we were paying for senior expertise and receiving work done by people three years into their careers. As practitioners, we knew how much of the value in this work sits in judgement that cannot be delegated — deciding which position to take, how much documentation is enough, and when a technically correct answer is commercially unusable.

Elite Finance Solutions is built around that observation. The firm stays deliberately small. Both partners work directly on files. We take on the number of engagements we can serve properly and decline the rest, and we tell clients when a matter genuinely belongs with a larger firm or is better handled in-house.

Our clients are Egyptian businesses that have outgrown a bookkeeping relationship, the Egyptian entities of European and Gulf-headquartered groups, and individuals with cross-border tax positions that a general practitioner will not touch.

How we operate

Principles we do not trade away

01

Independence of view

We give the answer we believe is right, including when it is not the one the client hoped for. Positions we cannot defend, we do not sign.

02

Documented reasoning

Every position we take is written down with the authority relied on, so that it can be explained by someone else long after we have left the room.

03

Confidentiality

Client information stays with the two of us. We do not publish client names, engagement details or case studies without express written consent.

04

Scope honesty

Fees are agreed in writing before work begins. If scope changes, we tell you before the hours are incurred, not on the invoice.

05

Continuity

The partner who scopes your work is the partner who does it and the partner who answers the phone in three years when the file is reopened.

06

Knowing our limits

We refer work that sits outside our competence or capacity. A referral costs us a fee; getting it wrong costs the client far more.

Work with a partner, not a process

Whether you need a second opinion, an annual compliance cycle or help through an inspection, the conversation starts the same way.
